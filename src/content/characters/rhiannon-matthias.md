## Background and Origins

Rhiannon comes from Morraine Province, a village on the Cygnisen border about an hour's walk from the outpost at Montserrat. That geography matters more than it first looks: it means her family sits close enough to Basgiath that she can reach them on foot, and the series uses exactly that to show what she left behind.

She is a twin. Her sister Raegan was pregnant when Rhiannon entered the Riders Quadrant, and by the time Rhiannon and Violet slip out of the Montserrat outpost to visit, Raegan has given birth to a son, Lukas. [FACT: 父母在书里怎么写的、家里其他成员]. The visit is one of the few scenes in *Fourth Wing* set outside Basgiath, and it does the work of explaining why she fights: not for glory, but for people who are still alive at the end of the week.

## Personality and Relationships

Rhiannon is written as loyal first and brave second. She is the one who promises to wait on the other side of the parapet before she has any reason to care about Violet, and she is the one who later tells Violet plainly that she is her only friend in the quadrant. She does not soften bad news — she trains Violet in exchange for history lessons, and she says what she thinks about the decisions Violet keeps to herself.

Her loyalty is not obedience. [FACT: 她当面顶撞权威的 1–2 个场面——这是她区别于“好人配角”的地方]

## Story Arc: Fourth Wing

Rhiannon meets Violet on the parapet — the crossing every candidate has to survive on day one — and tells her she will be waiting on the other side. That single line sets the tone for the rest of the book. She becomes the first person in the Riders Quadrant to offer Violet unqualified help, and the friendship is built on an exchange rather than charity: Rhiannon teaches Violet to fight, Violet teaches Rhiannon history.

The friction is real. When Violet clears the Gauntlet without telling anyone her plan, Rhiannon's reaction is not just relief but hurt — she had been left out. [FACT: 这场和好的具体过程]. She cries when Violet makes it through, which tells you more about her than any line of dialogue.

Later in the book she asks Violet for something harder: help slipping out of the Montserrat outpost to see Raegan and the baby. Violet agrees without hesitation. [FACT: 这一趟的结局与代价]

## Story Arc: Iron Flame

*Iron Flame* promotes her. Rhiannon becomes a squad leader, and the role change is visible in how she talks — she starts carrying the consequences of other people's choices rather than only her own. [FACT: 升任的具体场景]

She also becomes one of the loudest pro-rebellion voices inside the quadrant. [FACT: 她支持叛乱的 1–2 个关键场面] Her loyalty in this book stops being purely personal: she questions orders when they put her people at risk.
