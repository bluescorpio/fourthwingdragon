Violet Sorrengail is the protagonist of Rebecca Yarros's Empyrean series, which begins
with *Fourth Wing*. Born into Navarre's leading military family and raised to be a scribe,
she is forced into the Riders Quadrant and becomes the first rider in history to bond two
dragons — the black morningstartail Tairn and the golden feathertail Andarna.

## Who Violet Sorrengail Is

Violet is the third and youngest child of General Lilith Sorrengail, commanding general of
Navarre and commander of Basgiath War College, and Lieutenant Colonel Asher Sorrengail, a
scribe. She was born in July 613 AU, after her brother Brennan and sister Mira.

A fever her mother contracted during pregnancy left Violet with a connective tissue
disorder: her joints subluxate, her skin bruises and tears easily, and her bones break
under strain that would leave others unharmed. The series never names the condition, but
it closely mirrors Ehlers-Danlos syndrome — and Yarros has said the character's experience
draws on her own.

At two years old she was carried to the isle of Unnbriel to be dedicated to Dunne, the
goddess of war. The dedication was never completed: an oracle declared that Violet held
several possible destinies and could not be bound to one. She carries one visible trace of
it — brown hair that turns metallic silver from roughly shoulder length down, a colour
that resists cutting because new growth follows the same pattern.

Her father shaped how she thinks. Asher filled her childhood with books and trained her as
a scribe, taught her to verify sources and hunt for first-hand accounts, and introduced her
to *The Fables of the Barren* — the forbidden text that treated venin and wyvern as myth.
He died of heart failure about a year before *Fourth Wing* begins, after years of grief over
Brennan's reported death at the Battle of Aretia, when Violet was fourteen.

## Before the Riders Quadrant

The spring before Conscription Day, Lilith informed her daughter that no child of hers
would enter the Scribe Quadrant. Under Major Gillstead, Violet pivoted to physical training
for the Riders Quadrant and placed in the upper quarter for speed and agility on her
entrance assessment.

One earlier relationship belongs here, because it explains part of her caution. In June
631 AU, at eighteen, she began her first serious relationship — with Prince Halden Tauri.
It lasted seven months, ending when she found him in bed with one of his professors. His
explanation invoked grief over his brother Alic's death at Threshing. The relationship
ended there.

## Signets and Powers

**Lightning Wielding** is her primary signet, channelled through Tairn. It was one of the
last first-year signets to emerge. Felix Gerault's explanation is that her power is pure
energy which takes the shape of lightning because that is the form she instinctively
reaches for — which is why it responds so violently to her temper, and why hitting a
target took months of training.

[FACT: 觉醒那一场到底发生在哪一幕——两个来源打架：empyreanriders 说是在 War Games 里对
Jack Barlowe 出手时爆发，Audible 说是在 Basgiath 的一场对练中。这是全页最需要你对原著确认的
一句，写错会被粉丝直接指出来]

**Dream Walking** is her second signet, channelled through Andarna. It lets her enter and
move through a sleeping person's dreamscape. It is classed as a form of inntinnsic — the
family of signets that touch other minds — and it is unusually dangerous because a sleeping
subject cannot raise mental shields. It surfaced in *Iron Flame* and became fully clear in
*Onyx Storm*. She has walked in Xaden's dreams, and in those of a woman named Maren.

## Does Violet Have Three Signets?

This is the question readers ask most, and the answer is two — plus one borrowed gift.

- **Lightning wielding** — a true signet, from Tairn.
- **Dream walking** — a true signet, from Andarna.
- **Time manipulation** — not a signet. It was a temporary gift from Andarna's feathertail
  youth, letting Violet briefly suspend the flow of time. It was consumed at the Battle of
  Resson, when using it too long forced Andarna into accelerated adolescence. Neither of
  them kept it once Andarna entered her dreamless sleep.

So: two dragons, two signets, one gift that no longer exists.

## Violet's Dragons

**Tairneanach — "Tairn"** — a black morningstartail, one of the largest dragons in the
timeline and a little over a century old. He bonds with her after she steps between him
and three cadets to shield a smaller dragon. He is mated to Sgaeyl, Xaden's dragon, which
is why Violet and Xaden share a telepathic link before either of them wanted one — and why
neither of them can afford the other's death, since a rider dies with their dragon and a
mated dragon dies with its mate. Tairn calls her *Silver One*, and describes her as both
worthy and stubborn, with equal sincerity.

**Andarnaurram — "Andarna"** — a gold feathertail who bonds with Violet after Violet
defends her from fellow cadets. Feathertails, Violet later learns, are not a species: they
are juvenile dragons that have not yet matured. After the dreamless sleep Andarna emerges
with scales that look black, and Violet assumes she is a black dragon — until the end of
*Iron Flame*, when she learns Andarna is something else entirely: a member of a seventh
dragon type, the **Irids**.

## Weakness: The Body She Was Born In

Violet fights the entire series inside a body that is actively failing her. She wraps her
most vulnerable joints before sparring and flying, and she cannot hold her seat on a dragon
without a custom saddle — one Xaden commissions for Tairn.

Her second weakness is worse, and Major Varrish names it accurately: the people she loves.
Every threat that lands on her does so through them.

## Personality

Violet identifies herself as a scholar first. Professor Kaori tells her outright that she
surpasses both Brennan and Mira in intellect, and may also exceed them in compassion.

That compassion shows in how she fights. She aims for joints rather than organs, uses
non-fatal poisons, disables instead of kills, and refuses Tairn's suggestion to finish an
unconscious opponent at Threshing. Her first kill leaves her shaken — she cannot reconcile
what her power can do with what she believes strength is for.

She is also, stubbornly, not deferential. She argues with her mother, her wingleader, and
her own dragons. The arc across three books is the move from a first-year cadet who doubts
her own legitimacy to a second-year who can absorb the possibility that she is a form of
inntinnsic without flinching.

## Story Arc: Fourth Wing

Violet enters the Riders Quadrant against every instinct she has, and the first thing she
does is survive the Parapet. What follows is a year of being underestimated on purpose and
proven wrong by accident.

She bonds two dragons, which no rider has done, and inherits the political consequences of
both. She trains with Imogen, Rhiannon, Sawyer and Xaden. She makes an enemy of Jack
Barlowe. By the end of the year she is at the Battle of Resson, where she is stabbed with a
venin-poisoned blade — and wakes days later to find Brennan, alive, mending her.

## Story Arc: Iron Flame

*Iron Flame* is the book where everything she was raised on turns out to be a lie. Her
mother and Navarrian command have hidden the venin threat for decades; her brother has been
alive and leading the Revolution.

Violet returns to Basgiath and has to lie to everyone she cares about. She is captured and
tortured by Major Varrish, and Xaden saves her by revealing the truth about venin to the
student body. She then leaves for Aretia with the Revolution and studies ward magic — work
that becomes the only thing standing between Basgiath and a venin army.

At the wardstone she nearly dies trying to raise the wards. Lilith arrives and completes
them, sacrificing her own life for her daughter's. And in the last pages Xaden channels
earth magic and turns himself venin.

## Story Arc: Onyx Storm

*Onyx Storm* is a search for a cure and a hunt for a prophecy. Navarre seeks alliances, and
Violet hunts for a way to pull Xaden back from venin before he loses his soul.

The prophecy she has carried since she was two turns out to be about someone else: it was
Xaden, not Violet, who was its subject. She fights a decisive battle, and Xaden channels to
save her at the cost of his own soul. She wakes hours later with no memory of the battle,
learning that she asked for her memory to be erased — and that she is now married to Xaden
and Duchess of Tyrrendor.

## Fan Debates

**Is dream walking a signet or a crime?** Inntinnsic signets are grounds for execution in
Navarre. Readers argue about whether Violet's second signet makes her what the kingdom
hunts. [FACT: 书里有没有正面处理这一点，哪一幕]
